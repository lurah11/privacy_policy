2. Information stored on the device

The apps may store information needed for their features, such as settings, preferences, learning or gameplay progress, diary or book content, images, attachments, and other content entered or selected by the user. This information is stored locally within the app or in a location selected by the user.

The apps do not upload this user-created content to servers operated by AZ_ModestSoft and do not provide AZ_ModestSoft-operated account registration or cloud synchronization.

3. Information collected or shared by AZ_ModestSoft

The apps are designed so that AZ_ModestSoft does not collect or receive user-created app content, personal information entered into the apps, precise location, contacts, messages, photos, files, or app-usage analytics.

The apps do not sell or share user-created content with advertisers or data brokers. They do not use AZ_ModestSoft-operated advertising, analytics, or profiling services.

Data handled entirely on the user's device is not available to AZ_ModestSoft. Features or third-party services added in a future version will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy and in the applicable Google Play Data safety declaration.

4. Device permissions

Depending on the app and the feature used, an app may request access to device functions such as notifications, files, photos, media, or the camera. Such access is used only to provide the feature requested by the user, for example selecting an image, attaching a file, exporting content, or scheduling a local reminder.

Permission choices can be reviewed or changed through Android system settings. Denying an optional permission may prevent the related feature from working but should not affect unrelated features.

5. Exporting, backing up, and sharing

Some apps may allow the user to export, back up, print, or share content. The app transfers that content only when the user initiates the action and chooses a destination, application, storage location, or recipient.

After content leaves the app, its handling is controlled by the selected destination or third-party application and is subject to that party's privacy practices. Copies stored in device backups, shared folders, removable storage, or third-party cloud services must be deleted separately by the user.

Android or Google may also provide device-level backup features for some covered apps depending on the app, the user's device, and platform settings. Such services are operated by the relevant platform provider, not by AZ_ModestSoft.

ArithmaDrill-specific sharing and backup behavior

ArithmaDrill stores exercise profiles, settings, results, and progress locally. When the user chooses to share a result, ArithmaDrill creates an anonymous result image locally and passes it to an application selected by the user through Android's sharing interface. AZ_ModestSoft does not receive a copy. The selected application processes the image according to its own privacy policy.

The anonymous result image contains the displayed score and arithmetic configuration. It does not contain a user or child name, custom profile name, account or device identifier, exercise date or time, individual questions or answers, complete exercise history, purchase information, or user-entered free-form text.

ArithmaDrill can also export exercise history as a CSV file. Unlike the anonymous result image, this user-requested export intentionally contains the selected exercise and answer history. ArithmaDrill transfers either file only after the user confirms the action and chooses a receiving application. Cancelling Android's sharing interface sends nothing.

ArithmaDrill disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its private app data. Profiles, settings, history, and progress therefore remain on the current device and are not restored automatically after uninstalling the app or moving to another device. A user may use the CSV export feature when a portable copy of exercise history is wanted.

6. Google Play and in-app purchases

Some covered apps may offer optional purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. The apps and AZ_ModestSoft do not receive or store the user's credit-card number, bank-account details, Google Play balance credentials, or other payment-method credentials.

To provide, verify, or restore a purchased feature, an app may receive limited purchase information from Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase status, purchase token, or order identifier. In an offline implementation, this information is used for entitlement management and may be stored locally on the device.

AZ_ModestSoft may be able to view limited sales and transaction records made available by Google in Google Play Console, such as the purchased product, order identifier, date, price, currency, country, refund status, or transaction status. This information may be used for customer support, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal compliance. Payment processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy practices.

ArithmaDrill uses internet connectivity for Google Play Premium price lookup, purchase, restoration, and ownership reconciliation. Verified Premium access is cached locally so previously verified access and the app's core arithmetic practice, history, and analytics can continue to work offline. ArithmaDrill does not send purchase or entitlement information to an AZ_ModestSoft-operated server.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app data remains on the user's device until the user deletes it using an in-app function, clears the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstalls the app. The exact effect of uninstalling may depend on device backup and restore settings.

For ArithmaDrill specifically, private app data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Clearing its app storage or uninstalling it removes its local profiles, settings, history, and progress without automatic restoration by ArithmaDrill.

The covered apps do not require an AZ_ModestSoft-operated account. Therefore, there is no remote account or server-side user-content record for AZ_ModestSoft to delete. Google Play transaction records are retained and managed by Google and may also be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

8. Data security

Users should protect their devices with an appropriate screen lock and keep Android up to date. No storage method can guarantee absolute security, particularly on a rooted, compromised, shared, or unlocked device.
