About AZ ModestSoft

Made small.
Thought through.

We are an independent software developer building mini desktop and mobile apps and games

Our approach

Useful software does not have to be oversized. We start with one real need, keep the interface focused, and build only what helps the person using it.

Our apps are designed around the device in your hand or on your desk. Core features work offline, personal information stays local whenever possible, and an account is not required just to get something done.

Our games mainly focus on simple puzzle that suitable for all ages , including you and your kids. :)

  • 01Focused

    One clear job, with fewer distractions between you and the result.

  • 02Offline-first

    Dependable core features, even when the connection is not.

  • 03Respectful

    No unnecessary accounts, tracking, or collection of your content.

  • 04Independent

    Carefully made and directly supported by a small developer.
